The 5 acts

Rules Act 1 - Recall SLA compliance audit

Per NHTSA 49 CFR 577 / KBA Rueckruf, Code-1 and Code-2 recalls must complete within the campaign window measured from owner-notification date. The rule lives in the ontology as SLABreachedRecall - a derived concept that re-runs against the current data automatically.

Speaker beat: Expected output: 19 SLA-breached Open recalls. Leader: IBS-2024-A (Continental MK C1 brake-booster firmware, 8 cases). Severity Code 1 cases (HVB-2024-A, 7) matter most per VIN. By centre, Cologne / Hamburg / Munich are the most exposed.

Expected result. Bars / rows / shape.

Graph Act 2 - Continental MK C1 defect cascade

Supplier-to-VIN propagation today is a six-system stitch (BMW AIR -> iLEAD -> dealer-network DB). The ontology walks it in a single PyRel query that joins supplier -> part -> bom_node -> vehicle -> owner -> service centre.

Speaker beat: Expected output: 67 affected VINs across three plant-date cohorts (Munich, Regensburg, Spartanburg). Regional rollup: EU 53, NA 12, LATAM 2. Top centres: Hamburg, Frankfurt, Berlin, Munich. The Sankey shows the cascade visually.

Heuristic Act 3 - Per-VIN urgency ranking (top 20)

Score = w1*mileage + w2*notification_age + w3*accident_severity + w4*distance_to_centre. All weights and all inputs are PyRel-derived properties. Auditable, defensible, reproducible across runs.

Speaker beat: Top of the queue mixes Regensburg X1 M35i + iX1 units with high mileage and prior collisions, plus Dingolfing iX / i7 EVs from the HVB campaign. Visible accident_severity column makes the safety argument explicit.

Note: the predictive reasoner (GNN) is preview. This is a deterministic fit-score expressed in PyRel.

Prescriptive Act 4 - MIP: assign recall jobs to centres x 4 weeks

HiGHS MIP. Decision: x[recall, centre, week] binary. Constraints: per-centre weekly technician hours, per-centre per-campaign per-week parts stock, tooling-certification eligibility (HV / IBS / body shop). Objective: sum(x * urgency * (week-1)).

Speaker beat: Expected: OPTIMAL in under 60 seconds (warm). All 121 Open jobs scheduled. The binding constraint in practice is parts stock on the IBS campaign (Continental can't ship enough booster ECUs in 4 weeks). EU jobs cluster in DE centres; US in Spartanburg / Dallas / NYC / LA. Budget 2-3 min of dead air for the solver - use it to narrate.

Persistent rule Act 5 - Persistent rule: prior-accident VINs go to week 1 or 2

The rule is captured once as RecallAssignment.priority_flag on the ontology. Adding it triggers a re-solve under the new constraint sum(assign at week>=3) == 0 for priority VINs. The rule survives the session - the next agent / SE / human inherits it.

Speaker beat: Expected: re-solve OPTIMAL in under 60s. 4-6 reassignments (priority VINs move into weeks 1 or 2; other jobs absorb the slip). Total weighted lateness rises ~5-10% - the rule trades raw efficiency for safety, as intended. Live-add the rule. Re-solve. Audience sees the answer flip.

If things break

  • Engine not READY. .venv/bin/rai reasoners list to confirm. The gate auto-resumes them.
  • Stale notebook in Snowsight. .venv/bin/python prep_demo.py --skip-chat --skip-figures re-uploads it.
  • Agent answering with stale schema. .venv/bin/python -m agent.deploy update refreshes sprocs without re-registering.
  • Solver returning INFEASIBLE. Not a runtime problem - your formulation drifted. Check data/cars_demo_validation.sql first; if the data changed under you, regenerate. Otherwise the formulation needs /rai-prescriptive-problem-formulation.
  • Snowflake credentials. snow connection test -c rai. Profile lives in ~/.snowflake/connections.toml.
